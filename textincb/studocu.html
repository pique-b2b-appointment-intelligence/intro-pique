CTAs push access, not outcomes

No email capture: visitors leave without any retargeting

Across every page examined — homepage, premium page, and pricing page — there is no email form, lead magnet, or newsletter sign-up. Studocu drives significant organic traffic from students who are still on the fence about a paid subscription, and lets all of it walk away.
