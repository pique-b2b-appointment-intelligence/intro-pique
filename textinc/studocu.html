03

I looked at user growth versus growth in paying subscribers.

Studocu grew from 15 million users in 2021 to 60 million monthly users today. That scale was built through organic reach and platform network effects. What that number doesn't answer is how many of those 60 million users also have a paying relationship. Platforms at this scale facing a proposition shift like this always run into the same challenge: making sure content strategy and conversion paths keep pace with growth, instead of lagging behind.

The bottom line

The reach is there. Conversion is lagging behind the proposition.

What I see at Studocu is a pattern that comes up often with platforms that grew quickly through organic traffic. The content strategy was built for reach, and it delivered. But a proposition shift as fundamental as moving from document library to AI study partner requires the entire website to be realigned around that new promise. Not just the hero. Not just the product page. But also the hundreds of pages where traffic actually enters.
